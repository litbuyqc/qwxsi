03 / The shopping method

How to use an Oopbuy spreadsheet.

A repeatable process turns a long product list into a shortlist you can evaluate.

01

Choose a category

Start with a specific need: everyday sneakers, a layering piece, a matching set or an accessory. Set your preferred fit and budget.

02

Identify the exact variant

Record the product link, color, size and listing identifier. Similar photos can represent different materials or versions.

03

Check the evidence

Read measurements and seller terms. If inspection photos are available, compare them with the selected variant and written description.

04

Compare the full cost

Include delivery, service fees and any applicable taxes or duties. Recheck current stock and terms before placing an order.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes for an Oopbuy-related shopping workflow. Individual lists vary in their sources, accuracy and update frequency.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent category directory and educational shopping guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, rather than an Oopbuy checkout.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact product variant, garment or shoe measurements, material details, seller information, return terms and estimated delivered cost. Record missing information before deciding.

Do product photos prove authenticity or availability?

No. Reference photos alone do not establish authenticity, seller authorization or current stock. Verify the actual listing and supporting information before purchasing.

Does the listed price include international delivery?

Do not assume it does. Check item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international delivery and any applicable taxes or duties. Use current destination-specific quotes.

Can I browse without downloading a spreadsheet?

Yes. Use the category links on this page to browse CAFANS directly. This page provides a static directory and shopping guidance, with no spreadsheet download required.
